Master Services Agreement: Gulf Industrial Services
Contract ID: CT-1001

Scope:
Gulf Industrial Services provides maintenance, turnaround planning, emergency
repair crews, and rotating equipment inspection services for manufacturing sites.

Commercial terms:
Annual contract value is USD 1,850,000. Emergency call-out rates are included
for the first 120 hours each contract year. Additional emergency hours are billed
at USD 240 per hour.

Renewal and termination:
The agreement auto-renews for one additional 12-month term unless SABIC gives
written notice at least 120 days before the end date. Termination for convenience
requires 90 days notice. Termination for uncured safety breach requires 15 days
notice after written warning.

Risk clauses:
Supplier must maintain USD 10,000,000 liability insurance and name SABIC as an
additional insured party. Supplier must provide an annual site safety audit and
submit remediation actions within 30 days. Repeated failure to meet safety KPIs
is a material breach.

Data and confidentiality:
Supplier may receive operational schedules and site access rosters. The supplier
must keep all SABIC operational information confidential for five years after
contract termination.
