Data Processing Addendum: Nexa Cloud Analytics
Contract ID: CT-1003

Scope:
Nexa Cloud Analytics provides a hosted analytics platform, dashboards, and
support for contract performance and procurement data. The service processes
business contact details, usage logs, support tickets, and limited procurement
metadata.

Data residency and transfer:
All EU personal data and EU procurement metadata must be stored and processed in
EU-hosted environments. Cross-border transfer outside the EU requires prior
written approval from SABIC Legal and a documented transfer impact assessment.

Security controls:
Nexa must complete quarterly penetration testing and provide a summary report to
SABIC within 10 business days of completion. Critical vulnerabilities must be
remediated within 15 calendar days. High vulnerabilities must be remediated
within 30 calendar days.

Renewal and exit:
The agreement auto-renews unless SABIC gives written notice at least 180 days
before the end date. On exit, Nexa must provide a complete data export within 20
business days and certify deletion of residual data within 45 calendar days.

Commercial note:
The annual subscription is USD 620,000. Service credits apply if monthly uptime
falls below 99.5 percent.
